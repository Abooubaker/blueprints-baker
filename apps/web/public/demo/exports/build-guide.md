## Wiring order

1. Power off and disconnect the battery before touching any conductor.
2. Mount U1 (ESP32 DevKit V1) on insulated standoffs and keep USB and header pins clear.
3. Mount U2 (TB6612FNG dual motor driver) so the heatsink has airflow and the motor terminals cannot short against the chassis.
4. Build the ground bus first: BT1.NEG to a single star point, then to every GND pin. BT1 negative is the only reference.
5. Connect BT1.POS through a fuse to the motor supply and buck input. Leave the battery unplugged until the continuity check passes.
6. Wire the motor outputs: driver AO1/AO2 to the first motor, BO1/BO2 to the second. Keep the pair twisted; reverse one pair if a wheel spins backwards.
7. Connect the controller PWM and direction pins to PWMA/AIN1/AIN2 and PWMB/BIN1/BIN2. Tie STBY high through the logic rail.
8. Wire sensors last, following the pin map one net at a time. Power 3.3 V-only sensors from the 3.3 V rail and fit a level shifter where flagged.
9. Inspect for stray strands, reversed polarity, and any pin assigned to two nets.
10. Power on with a current-limited supply or the fuse fitted. Measure the 5 V rail under load before enabling the motors.
11. Run each actuator at low duty first, then verify emergency stop and battery low-voltage cutoff before a full-speed test.
